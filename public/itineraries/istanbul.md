## Travel Notes

**Pass strategy:** Your 2-month continuous Interrail Global Pass is already booked (£359 sale price). Check its start date is 17 Aug. Reserve the Eurostar (London–Paris on 17 Aug and Paris–Brussels on 19 Aug), both TGV Lyria legs (Paris–Lausanne on 18 Aug, Lausanne–Paris on 19 Aug) and the Brussels–Strasbourg TGV (21 Aug) as early as the booking window opens — pass-holder quotas are limited. The Brussels–Strasbourg direct TGV runs only about twice a day; if it's full, the fallback is Brussels → Luxembourg (IC, \~3h) → Strasbourg (TER, \~2–2h30, usually changing at Metz), about 5h30–6h in total with no reservations needed. The two Balkan buses are bought separately (FlixBus/Arriva) and aren't on the pass.

**Diamond League nights:** Book the Lausanne, Brussels and Zurich tickets and hotels first; they are the hardest dates to flex and the nights most likely to sell out or spike in price. Tickets and on-sale dates come from each meeting's own organiser, so check their sites.

**Switzerland:** Everything is pricey, so a supermarket picnic helps. Lausanne and Zurich are both meeting nights at peak prices; Colmar and Freiburg either side are noticeably cheaper bases. Interrail gives discounts on some mountain railways and lake boats.

**Overnight sleepers (Istanbul legs):**

- **Only route:** The Sofia–Istanbul Express is the one daily, year-round train linking Europe with Istanbul (sleeper and couchette cars). Bucharest is not a separate route: a direct Bucharest–Istanbul couchette car runs only June–October, and outside that season you connect via Dimitrovgrad in Bulgaria onto the Sofia train.
- **Check for 2027:** Your 12 Sep return to Bucharest falls inside the seasonal window, but 2027 timetables aren't out yet. Re-check seat61.com in spring; if the direct car isn't running, use the Dimitrovgrad connection instead.
- **Booking:** These trains generally can't be reserved in the Interrail app. Seat61's "how to buy tickets" section lists the options, including emailing an agent. Online booking of the Sofia/Dimitrovgrad–Istanbul sleeper reportedly opened in Feb 2026, but it's unclear whether that works with a pass, and one traveller reports pass fares from Istanbul can only be bought in person. The train often leaves fully booked in summer and autumn, so arrange both reservations well ahead rather than relying on the station.
- **Istanbul station:** Both trains use Halkalı, about 25 km west of the centre. Allow time for the Marmaray suburban train into the city (buy an Istanbulkart).
- **Sofia timing:** The Istanbul train leaves Sofia in the evening (around 21:00). After checking out on Tue 7 Sep you'll have a day to fill, so plan to use left luggage at the station.
- **Durations & passports:** Sofia→Istanbul is \~11h and Istanbul→Bucharest roughly 18h (unconfirmed, as the 2027 times aren't published). Each leg saves a hotel night. Passport checks happen mid-journey, often in the small hours — keep your passport accessible.

**Currencies:**

- UK — pound (GBP); card everywhere.
- France, Germany, Austria, Netherlands, Belgium, Croatia, **Bulgaria** — euro (EUR); card-dominant (Bulgaria adopted the euro on 1 Jan 2026; carry a little cash there).
- Switzerland — Swiss franc (CHF); cards widely accepted, so little cash needed.
- Serbia — dinar (RSD); carry some cash.
- Türkiye — lira (TRY); cash handy in bazaars, cards widely accepted.
- Romania — leu (RON); cash useful outside cities.
- Hungary — forint (HUF); cards common.
- Poland — złoty (PLN); cards common.

**Language:** English is widely spoken in tourist areas throughout. French is useful in Paris, Lausanne, Brussels, Strasbourg and Colmar, and German in Freiburg, Zurich, Munich and Austria. A few local greetings go a long way — "merhaba" (Turkish), "здравей/zdravey" (Bulgarian), "cześć" (Polish).

**Weather:** Mid-to-late August in Switzerland and southern Germany is warm, with afternoon thunderstorms possible. Late Aug/early Sep is warm in the Balkans and Türkiye (25–30°C); by late September the north (Berlin, Amsterdam) cools to 10–15°C. Pack layers, a waterproof, and a modest cover-up (scarf/shawl) for mosque visits in Istanbul.

**Booking platforms:** seat61.com for route planning; the Interrail app for timetables and reservations; national operators where needed (SBB, ÖBB, DB, CFR Călători, BDŽ, TCDD) and FlixBus for the Balkan coach legs.
