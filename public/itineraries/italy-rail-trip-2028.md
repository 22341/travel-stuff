### Phase 3: Campania

- **Salerno:** 2 nights (by train) _Arrive: Mon 26 Jun 2028_

- **Home:** Naples → Manchester (by flight) _Sat 1 Jul 2028_

## Visa Requirements

- No visa needed for UK passport holders for stays up to 90 days in any 180-day period
- **ETIAS** travel authorisation is expected to be mandatory by 2028 — apply online via the official EU site only (beware lookalike sites); check fee and timings closer to travel
- **EES** biometric registration (fingerprints and photo) on first entry to the Schengen area
- Passport must be issued within the last 10 years and valid for at least 3 months after the return date

## Travel Notes

### Pass Strategy

- An Interrail pass is unlikely to be worth it — most legs are short and cheap
- Book Frecciarossa / Italo legs (Verona → Bologna, Bologna → Florence, Rome → Salerno) in advance for the cheapest fares; they're released around 4 months ahead
- Regional trains are fixed-price and don't need booking ahead — validate paper tickets before boarding

### Currencies

- **Euro (€):** cards and contactless accepted almost everywhere; carry some cash for small bars, buses and tourist tax, especially in Campania

### Language

- English widely spoken in hotels and tourist areas, less so in Naples and Salerno
- Useful words: _grazie_ (thank you), _per favore_ (please), _il conto_ (the bill), _una birra artigianale_ (a craft beer), _binario_ (platform)

### Weather

- Mid-June to early July: north ~25–30°C, Tuscany and Campania ~28–33°C, with Florence often hotter in late June
- Pack light, breathable clothing, sun protection and a refillable water bottle; cover shoulders and knees for churches

### Booking Platforms

- **Trenitalia** and **Italo** for intercity trains (Trainline also works)
- **EAV Circumvesuviana** for Pompeii and Herculaneum from Naples
- **Travelmar** ferries for Salerno → Amalfi / Positano
- **Tiemme** buses for Siena → Montalcino
