## Visa Requirements

Spain is in the Schengen Area. UK passport holders can visit visa-free for up to 90 days in any 180-day period — this 14-night trip is well within that limit. Make sure passports are valid for at least 3 months beyond the return date.

## Travel Notes

**Currencies:** Euro (€) throughout. Cards are widely accepted in cities, but smaller tapas bars and the Ronda white villages can be more cash-reliant — worth carrying some cash.

**Language:** Spanish. English is common in central Granada, Seville and Málaga's tourist areas, less so in Córdoba, Cádiz and Ronda — a few basic Spanish phrases go a long way.

**Weather:** Mid-to-late September in Andalucía is warm — expect daytime highs of 27–31°C inland (Córdoba, Seville) and slightly cooler on the coast (Cádiz, Málaga), with evenings around 19–21°C. Pack light layers, sun protection, and comfortable shoes for cobbled old towns.

**Driving notes:** Historic centres in Granada, Córdoba and Seville have restricted traffic zones (ZTL) — check hotel parking access before driving into the old town. Ronda's mountain roads are narrow but well maintained.
